# Student survey

Link: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeH-tnpke1X1or_hZ-ziDnn6M8Xsh-z6rOJmvfZmpGaoIWi3A/viewform
Opened: 28 Oct 2026. Originally planned to close: 4 Nov 2026. Responses analysed: 19 Nov 2026.
Anonymous, no names or emails. For international students at Taylor's.

## Purpose
We want to know how international students handle money in two currencies (MYR and their home currency), and which features they would use. The answers support our requirements and our user tests.

## Questions
* What is your main source of money?
* Which currency do you usually receive your money in?
* How do you track your spending now?
* How often do you check exchange rates?
* What is the most difficult part of managing your money?
* Would you use an app that automatically sorts your expenses?
* Would you use an app that predicts how much money you will have at the end of the month?
* Would you use an app that alerts you when the exchange rate is good?
* Would you let a parent or sponsor see your budget (read-only)?

### What this means for Student Wallet

Of the 20 respondents, 16 (80%) selected family as their main source of money, and six (30%) said they do not track their spending. Automatic expense sorting received 13 Yes answers (65%), exchange-rate alerts received 11 (55%), and the balance forecast received 10 (50%). These answers support keeping those features in the prototype, although stated interest does not establish how often students would use them. Sponsor access received five Yes answers (25%), eight Maybe answers (40%) and seven No answers (35%), which suggests that access should stay optional and under the student’s control. The sample is small and self-selected, so the findings describe these respondents rather than all international students at Taylor’s.
