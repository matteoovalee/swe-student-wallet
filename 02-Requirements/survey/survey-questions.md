Link: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeH-tnpke1X1or_hZ-ziDnn6M8Xsh-z6rOJmvfZmpGaoIWi3A/viewform
Opened: 28 Oct 2026. Closes: 4 Nov 2026.
Anonymous, no names or emails. For international students at Taylor's.

## Purpose
We want to know how international students handle money in two currencies (MYR and their home currency), and which features they would use. The answers support our requirements and our user tests.

## Questions
* What is your main source of money?
* Which currency do you usually receive your money in?
* How do you track your spending now?
* How often do you check exchange rates?
* What is the most difficult part of managing your money?
* Would you use an app that automatically sorts your expenses?
* Would you use an app that predicts how much money you will have at the end of the month?
* Would you use an app that alerts you when the exchange rate is good?
* Would you let a parent or sponsor see your budget (read-only)?
