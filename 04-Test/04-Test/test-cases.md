# Student Wallet Test Cases

Use demo accounts and fake transactions. Where timing or exchange rates affect the result, use the fixture described in the test and record the environment. Actual results below record execution evidence from22–23 November2026. Pass may combine manual and explicitly identified controlled checks; limitations are retained. Unimplemented features remain Not implemented. Export (FR-08) and sponsor invitations (FR-10) are not part of the prototype and are marked Not implemented. Registration, part of FR-13, is not built either: the prototype has three demo accounts (student, sponsor, admin).
